01 · STUCK

Waiting to feel ready
can keep you waiting.

We tell ourselves we’ll begin when we feel motivated, confident or certain. But feelings are unreliable starting lines.

Move your cursor through what’s holding you back.

02 · ONE STEP

First you move.
Then something shifts.

Breakthrough reverses the order. You don’t wait for the feeling. You choose one small action you can take today.

Feelings can come along for the ride. They don’t have to drive.

03 · REPETITION

Small doesn’t mean
insignificant.

One action can feel almost too small to matter. Repeated action starts becoming evidence: “I am someone who can move even when this is hard.”

04 · CHANGE

Forty days.
Not forty perfect days.

The goal isn’t a life without struggle. It’s a different relationship with struggle. More room. More choice. More movement.

Drag the whole form. It is still moving. It is no longer stuck.

THIS ISN’T ABOUT TECHNOLOGY

The struggle can
still be there.
And you can still move.

Breakthrough combines practical CBT-based tools with one deliberately simple commitment: stop measuring progress only by how you feel.

Notice what is happening. Choose what matters. Take the next small step. Then come back tomorrow.

DAY ONE CAN BE TODAY

Forty days won’t
make you perfect.

They can make you different.
