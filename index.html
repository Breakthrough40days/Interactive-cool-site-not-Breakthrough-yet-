A QUICK INTRODUCTION

HOW THE ROOM WORKS

The Room is an experiment in learning from you. It watches your movements, notices patterns, asks questions and tries to remember what you teach it.

  1. Start the camera.Allow camera access. You can also allow the microphone if asked.
  2. Move naturally.Try waving, changing position or repeating a gesture. Give it a moment to observe.
  3. Respond.When the Room makes a guess, choose Yes, No or It depends. You can also type a question.
  4. Come back.On the same browser, the Room can use saved interaction history to recognize patterns.

Your privacy: Camera and microphone inputs are processed live. Derived interaction information may be sent to the Room AI. You can delete saved Room memory using More Options.

Interactive browser experiments

Breakthrough Interactive Lab is a collection of experimental web experiences exploring hand tracking, browser camera interaction, microphone audio visualization, device motion, persistent touch memory and GPU particle simulation. Camera and microphone experiences are designed to process live input in the browser rather than store recordings.

Experiments

Body + Matter lets hand movements interact with simulated particles. Memory + Touch creates persistent local traces. Voice + Matter turns live audio characteristics into interactive shapes. Device + Space responds to phone orientation and pointer movement. Action + Time visualizes repeated daily action across forty days. Body + Time creates a temporal mirror from live camera frames.
